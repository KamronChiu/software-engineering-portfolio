## Suggested method paragraph

The project adopted an appointment-level synthetic data generation approach to overcome the lack of access to real outpatient operational data. After cleaning the core patient and doctor tables, an expanded appointment dataset was generated using Python. Each synthetic appointment record preserved relational consistency with the patient and doctor master tables and included operational features such as booking date, appointment date, lead time, day of week, time slot, appointment type, reminder status, weather condition, and no-show outcome. The no-show label was not assigned randomly. Instead, it was generated by an interpretable probability rule in which the risk increased with longer lead time, higher historical no-show tendency, travel distance, rainy weather, and selected temporal patterns, while reminder delivery and follow-up status reduced the risk. This design ensured that the synthetic data were suitable for both predictive modelling and later scheduling simulation.
